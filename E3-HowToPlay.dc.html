The leader picks a team

Each round one player leads and picks who goes on the quest. Talk, accuse, defend. The leader role moves one seat clockwise after every vote.

Everyone votes, then it's public

Approve or Reject, in secret. Then every vote is shown by name. Five rejected teams in a row and Evil wins. Votes are your best clue: check History often.

First to three quests

Three Successes and Good is close to winning. Three Fails and Evil wins. With 7 or more players, Quest 4 needs two Fails to fail.

The final twist

Merlin knows Evil but must stay hidden. If Good wins three quests, the Assassin gets one guess at Merlin. Guess right and Evil steals the win.
